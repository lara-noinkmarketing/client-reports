# 🔒 KENT ISOLATION POLICY

## The Rule

**Kent's workflows and Google Sheets are COMPLETELY SEPARATE and UNTOUCHABLE.**

- ❌ Never edit Kent's workflows
- ❌ Never modify Kent's Google Sheets
- ❌ Never reference Kent's values in new client systems
- ❌ Never share Make credentials between Kent and other clients

## Why

1. **Kent's system is complex** — Integrated with his CRM, custom logic, specific requirements
2. **Kent's clients deserve stability** — His reports are production-critical
3. **New client system is simpler** — Designed for standard real estate reporting only
4. **Isolation = Safety** — If something breaks in new system, Kent is unaffected

## What We Can Do

✅ **Reference Kent's workflows** — Study them for best practices  
✅ **Use Kent as a blueprint** — Copy structure/patterns to new templates  
✅ **Learn from Kent's approach** — Apply lessons to our standard system  

## What We Can't Do

❌ **Edit Kent's Make scenarios** — Ever  
❌ **Modify Kent's Google Sheets** — Ever  
❌ **Reuse Kent's module configs** — Create new ones for standard clients  
❌ **Mix Kent with new client system** — Keep them entirely separate  

## Implementation

When building/onboarding new clients:
1. Use the **Client Template System** (this directory)
2. **Never touch** anything in Kent's Make workspace
3. Keep two separate systems:
   - **Kent System**: His custom workflows (off-limits)
   - **Standard Client System**: Templated workflows (your system)
